Our Design Process

We follow a structured approach to bring your vision to life, ensuring a smooth journey from concept to completion.

Initial Consultation

We begin with an in-depth conversation about your vision, needs, lifestyle, and budget. This helps us understand your expectations and establish project parameters.

  • Site assessment and measurements
  • Understanding functional requirements
  • Discussing aesthetic preferences
  • Establishing budget and timeline

Concept Development

Based on our initial consultation, we create preliminary design concepts that include mood boards, material selections, and space planning ideas.

  • Mood board creation
  • Material and color palette selection
  • Preliminary space planning
  • Concept presentation and feedback

Design Development

After concept approval, we refine the design with detailed floor plans, elevations, 3D visualizations, and comprehensive material specifications.

  • Detailed floor plans and elevations
  • 3D renderings of key spaces
  • Furniture and fixture selections
  • Material and finish specifications

Implementation

We coordinate with contractors and suppliers to execute the design, managing the process to ensure quality and adherence to the design vision.

  • Contractor coordination
  • Material procurement
  • Construction oversight
  • Quality control inspections

Final Styling

The final phase involves installing furniture, accessories, and artwork, adding those personal touches that make a house feel like home.

  • Furniture placement
  • Accessory styling
  • Art installation
  • Final walkthrough and handover
